Compressible Channel Flow

High-speed compressible turbulent channel flow at multiple friction Reynolds numbers and Mach numbers.

HYVES Ma 0.2 – 2.5 Reτ up to 2000 Channel Geometry 3D

Instantaneous Flow Field (Ma 0.8, Reτ 2000)

Case Setup / Grid Parameters

Multiple high-resolution direct numerical simulation cases of compressible channel flow are provided. All cases use 3D periodic boundary conditions in the streamwise and spanwise directions and no-slip, adiabatic walls.
