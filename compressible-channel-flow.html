Compressible Channel Flow

High-fidelity compressible turbulent channel flow at multiple Reynolds and Mach numbers.

Mab = 0.8 – 3.0 Reτ = 198 - 3668 Reτc* = 92 - 2846 3D

Instantaneous Flow Field (Mab = 1.5, Reb = 70,000)

Case Setup / Grid Parameters

Multiple high-fidelity direct numerical simulation cases of compressible turbulent channel flow are provided. All cases use periodic boundary conditions in the streamwise and spanwise directions and no-slip, isothermal walls.
