About the Database

Open turbulence data for open science. We are a global community building high-quality, open-access datasets to advance turbulence research.

Database Basics

OTD provides a unified access point to a growing collection of high-fidelity turbulence datasets and numerical simulations. All data are openly accessible, carefully documented, and organized to support reproducible and transparent research.

We focus on a wide range of flows, methods, and Reynolds numbers to enable comparative analysis and accelerate scientific discovery.

Mission

Provide a reliable, open-access repository of turbulence datasets to accelerate research and education across engineering, physics, and data science.

Vision

Become the global standard for open turbulence data, enabling reproducible science and empowering the next generation of discoveries.

Open Science

We believe that open data drives innovation. OTDB is freely accessible to all and built through the contributions of researchers worldwide.
