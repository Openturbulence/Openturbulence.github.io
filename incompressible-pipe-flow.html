Incompressible Pipe Flow

High-fidelity incompressible turbulent pipe flow spanning a wide range of friction Reynolds numbers.

Reb = 5,300 – 240,000 Reτ = 181 – 5197 3D

Instantaneous Flow Field (Reτ = 5197)
