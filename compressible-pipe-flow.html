Compressible Turbulent Pipe Flow

High-fidelity compressible turbulent pipe flow at multiple Reynolds and Mach numbers.

Mb = 1.5 – 3.0 Reτ = 233.1 - 514.5 Reτ,c* = 148.3 - 336.9 3D

Instantaneous Flow Field (Mb = 1.5, Reb = 7300)

Case Setup / Grid Parameters

Multiple high-fidelity direct numerical simulation cases of compressible turbulent pipe flow are provided. All cases use periodic boundary conditions in the axial direction and no-slip, isothermal pipe-wall boundary conditions.
